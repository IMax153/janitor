# Unslop

Rewrite once, then check the result:

- Lead with the fact or action.
- Prefer short active sentences and concrete nouns.
- Delete puffery, generic conclusions, fake quotations, and unsupported claims.
- Replace vague attribution with a named source or remove it.
- Remove repetitive framing, forced lists, synonym cycling, and chatbot filler.
- Avoid decorative headings, emojis, and long-dash punctuation.
- Keep necessary uncertainty and technical detail.
- Match the repository's existing voice.

Do not make prose less precise merely to make it shorter.
